Coalition of APC Support Groups Appoints Alhassan Mohammed Baba as National DG

Hon. Alhassan Mohammed Baba has been appointed the National Director-General of the Coalition of APC Support Groups.

The appointment was announced by the Convener of the coalition, Richard M. Onyebuwa, in Abuja.

Onyebuwa said the appointment was based on Baba’s experience, commitment and contributions to supporting the administration of President Bola Tinubu and the All Progressives Congress (APC).

He expressed confidence that Baba would provide effective leadership and coordinate the activities of the coalition’s support groups across the country.

Baba is a community development advocate and political mobiliser with experience in youth and grassroots engagement.
