The Director-General of the Coalition of APC Support Groups, Alhassan Mohammed Baba, says he will work to ensure President Bola Tinubu wins the 2027 presidential election.

Baba made the declaration while speaking on the activities of the coalition and its plans to mobilise support for the president ahead of the election.

He said the coalition would strengthen its grassroots structures and work with support groups across the country to promote the achievements of the Tinubu administration.

He called on members of the coalition to remain committed, united and focused as the group continues its mobilisation activities across the country.
