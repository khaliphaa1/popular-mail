The National Director General of the Coalition of APC Support Groups, Hon. Alhassan Mohammed Baba, has called on Nigerians to remain patient and supportive of President Bola Ahmed Tinubu, saying the administration has taken important steps to rebuild the country and strengthen the economy.

Baba said the President inherited a difficult situation but has demonstrated the political will to take difficult decisions necessary to put Nigeria on a stronger footing.

According to him, the removal of fuel subsidy, reforms in the foreign exchange market and other economic measures introduced by the Tinubu administration are already creating a foundation for sustainable growth, although the immediate effects have been difficult for many Nigerians.

He said the administration's efforts in infrastructure development, road construction, agriculture, social intervention programmes, education, healthcare and job creation also showed that the government was not focused only on economic reforms.

“President Tinubu has shown courage in taking decisions that previous administrations avoided. Some of these decisions are painful, but we must also look at where they are taking the country and what they can achieve in the long run,” Baba said.

The Coalition DG noted that the Federal Government had continued to respond to the concerns of Nigerians through various interventions aimed at reducing the impact of economic hardship, particularly among vulnerable families, young people, women and small businesses.

He said the administration's Renewed Hope agenda would require continuity, consistency and the cooperation of Nigerians to achieve its objectives.

Baba assured Nigerians that the Coalition of APC Support Groups would continue to engage citizens across the country, listen to their concerns and communicate the policies and programmes of the Federal Government to the grassroots.

He stressed that the Coalition was not established merely to defend the government, but also to ensure that the voices of ordinary Nigerians were heard and that the achievements and challenges of the administration were properly communicated.

The National Director-General called on APC supporters and members of the various support groups to remain united and conduct themselves responsibly, saying political support should be based on facts, development and the interest of Nigeria.

He further expressed confidence that President Tinubu would continue to introduce policies capable of strengthening the economy, improving infrastructure, creating opportunities for young Nigerians and positioning the country for greater development.

Baba urged Nigerians to continue supporting the administration as it implements its programmes, assuring them that the Coalition would remain committed to promoting good governance, national unity and development.
